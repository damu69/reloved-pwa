Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Reloved is operated by MIA Solutions (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what personal information the Reloved website and app collect, why, who sees it, and the choices you have. By using Reloved you agree to it. Reloved is for people aged 18 and over.

What we collect

InformationWhenWhy
Name, email, password (stored only as a secure hash), optional phone numberYou registerTo run your account and sign you in
Delivery addresses and phone numbersYou add an address or place an orderTo deliver your orders
Orders, cart, favourites, returns and refundsYou use the marketplaceTo process orders and keep records
Messages you send to buyers and sellers, and reports or blocks you makeYou chat or reportTo let you talk to other users and keep the community safe
Listings, descriptions and photosYou sellTo show your items to buyers
Seller verification: PAN, business and address details, address and bank proof documents, GSTIN if you have one, bank account detailsYou apply to sellTo verify sellers, meet tax and legal duties, and pay you. Documents and numbers are encrypted and visible only to the review team
Technical data: IP address, device and sign-in session information, security logsYou use the serviceTo keep accounts secure and prevent fraud

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and we do not run third-party advertising in the app.

Photos and camera

If you list an item, the app asks to use your camera or photo library so you can add pictures. We use them only for the listing you are creating.

Who we share it with

  • Sellers and buyers. A seller sees the name, delivery address and phone number needed to ship your order. Other users see your first name or shop name, your listings and the messages you send them.
  • Service providers that run Reloved for us: cloud hosting and database (Vercel, Supabase), email delivery (Resend), and a payment provider once online payments are switched on. They process data only to provide their service.
  • Authorities where the law requires it, or to protect users and prevent fraud.

We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is open. When you delete your account we remove your profile, addresses, favourites and cart and anonymise the account. Order, payment, invoice and seller tax records are kept for as long as the law requires, but no longer show your name or contact details. Security logs are kept for a limited period.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and data from the app (Profile, Settings, Delete my account) or see how to delete your account.
  • Correct your information in Profile, Settings and My addresses.
  • Ask for a copy of your information, or raise a concern, by emailing us at the address below.
  • Block or report a user from a chat, or report a listing from the item page.

Security

Connections are encrypted. Passwords are hashed, sensitive seller documents are encrypted, and access to them is limited and logged. No system is perfectly secure, so use a strong, unique password.

Children

Reloved is not for anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell you in the app.

Contact and grievances

Email contact@miasolutions.in for privacy questions, data requests or complaints. We aim to reply within 7 days.
