## Short description (max 80)
Buy and sell pre-owned fashion. Swipe through items and chat with sellers.

## Full description (max 4000)
Reloved is a marketplace for pre-owned fashion and lifestyle finds, from independent sellers across India.

SWIPE TO DISCOVER
Go through listings one card at a time. Swipe right to message the seller, left to skip, up to save it for later. Prefer to browse? Use search, categories and filters on the home screen.

CHAT WITH SELLERS
Ask about size, condition or delivery before you buy. Every conversation is about a specific item, so nothing gets lost.

SHOP WITH CONFIDENCE
Sellers are verified before they can list. Prices include GST. You see delivery and Buyer Protection fees before you order. Items that arrive damaged or fake can be returned within 7 days.

SELL YOUR OWN
Clear out your wardrobe or run a small shop. Apply as a seller, add your items with photos and sizes, manage orders and see your earnings in one place.

BUILT FOR SAFETY
Report listings and users, block anyone who bothers you, and delete your account any time from Settings.

What you can find: women's, men's and kids' clothing, footwear, bags, accessories and home decor.

Questions? Write to contact@miasolutions.in.
