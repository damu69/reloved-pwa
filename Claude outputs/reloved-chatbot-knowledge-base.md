# Reloved: Knowledge Base for the Support Chatbot

**How to use this file:** it is the single source of truth for answering customer questions about Reloved. Answer only from this file. If a question is not covered, say you are not sure and point the person to human support (see "Contact and escalation"). Never invent fees, dates, policies or features. Anything marked **(trial)** is not switched on yet; say so plainly rather than promising it.

Reloved is currently a **trial / early version**. Where this file says "not available yet", tell the customer it is coming and do not give a date.

---

## 1. About Reloved

**What is Reloved?**
Reloved is a marketplace for pre-owned (second-hand) fashion and lifestyle items in India. Many independent sellers list items, and buyers can browse, message sellers, and order. Prices are in Indian rupees (₹) and include GST.

**What can be sold?**
Clothing (women, men, kids), footwear, bags, accessories (watches, sunglasses, jewellery) and home décor. Sellers must own what they list and describe it honestly. Counterfeit, illegal or unsafe items are not allowed.

**Who can use it?**
Members must be 18 or over.

**Is Reloved a website or an app?**
Both. It opens in any browser (phone or laptop) and can be installed on a phone's home screen like an app. On a laptop it shows a wider layout with a top navigation bar. Same account everywhere.

**Is it free to use?**
Browsing, saving favourites and messaging sellers are free. Fees apply when you buy (see Section 6) and when you sell (see Section 9).

## 2. Getting started

**How do I create an account?**
Tap Profile (or any action that needs an account, such as adding to cart) and choose to register with your name, email and a password. You can browse without an account; you need one to add to cart, order, save favourites or message sellers.

**I forgot my password.**
Password reset by email is **not available yet (trial)**. Ask human support for help.

**How do I change my name, password or theme?**
Profile → Settings. You can also choose light or dark theme, and sign out of this device or all devices.

**How do I sign out?**
Profile → Settings → Sign out (this device) or Sign out everywhere.

## 3. Finding items

**What are the tabs at the bottom (or top on a laptop)?**
- **Home:** search, category and subcategory chips, filters.
- **Discover:** swipe through items one at a time.
- **Sell:** become a seller or manage your shop.
- **Inbox:** your chats with buyers and sellers.
- **Cart:** items you are about to buy.
- **Profile:** orders, favourites, addresses, settings, help.

**How do I search?**
Use the search bar on Home. Search tolerates small spelling mistakes. Use Filters to sort and to narrow by condition and price.

**What does the condition label mean?**
New with tags, New, Very good, Good, Satisfactory. The seller chooses the label and describes any flaws in the description.

**Why can't I see photos on some items?**
Sellers upload photos when they list. Items without a photo show a coloured tile with an icon. Demo listings (see Section 12) do not have photos.

**How do I save an item?**
Tap the heart on the item. Your saved items are in Profile → Favourite items.

## 4. Discover (swipe)

**How does Discover work?**
Items appear as cards. Swipe or press:
- **Right** (or → key): message the seller about the item.
- **Left** (or ← key): skip it.
- **Up** (or ↑ key): save it to favourites.
- Buttons under the card do the same, and an **Undo** button brings back the last card.

**What happens when I swipe right?**
A message box opens, pre-filled with a short hello. You can edit it and send, or cancel. Nothing is sent until you confirm. You must be signed in.

**Will skipped items come back?**
Skipped items are hidden on this device. Use the reset option on the Discover screen to see them again.

**Can I filter Discover?**
Yes, use the category chips at the top of the Discover screen.

## 5. Messaging sellers (chat)

**How do I message a seller?**
Open an item and tap **Message seller**, or swipe right in Discover. Chats appear in the **Inbox** tab. There is one conversation per buyer per item.

**Do I get notifications?**
Not yet **(trial)**. New messages show as a number on the Inbox tab and the chat refreshes every few seconds while it is open. There are no email or push alerts yet.

**Message limits and rules**
Messages are plain text, up to 1000 characters. There are limits on how many messages and new chats you can send per minute and per hour to prevent spam. You cannot message yourself about your own item.

**Can I send photos or make offers in chat?**
Not yet. Chat is text only.

**Safety tip for chat:** keep payment and conversation inside Reloved. Pay only through the app to keep Buyer Protection.

## 6. Buying: cart, checkout and fees

**How do I buy something?**
Open the item, choose an option (such as size) if there is one, and tap **Add to cart**. Go to Cart, choose delivery for each seller's package, apply a coupon if you have one, add or choose an address, and place the order.

**Can I buy from several sellers in one order?**
Yes. Each seller's items ship as a separate package with its own delivery fee and its own status.

**Can I buy my own item?**
No.

**How much does delivery cost?**
Charged per seller package. Choose in the cart:
- Home delivery: ₹99
- Pickup point: ₹59
- Meet and collect: free

**What is the Buyer Protection fee?**
₹15 plus 5% of the items' price (after any discount), charged once per order. It covers the Buyer Protection described in Section 8. The exact amount is shown before you place the order.

**Do prices include GST?**
Yes. Item prices include GST. Delivery and Buyer Protection fees are added at checkout.

**How do coupons work?**
Coupons are created by Reloved. Enter the code in the cart. One coupon per order. A coupon can be limited to certain categories or sellers; then it only discounts matching items. An invalid or expired code shows a message in the cart.

**How do I pay?**
Online payment is **not switched on yet (trial)**. You can place an order but it cannot be paid for yet. Unpaid orders are released automatically after **15 minutes** and the items go back on sale. Do not tell customers a payment method or date.

**Why was my order cancelled automatically?**
Because it was not paid within 15 minutes.

**Why did I get "only N left" or "no longer available"?**
Items are usually one-of-a-kind. Another buyer may have taken it, or the seller removed it. Items in someone's unpaid checkout are held for a short time.

**Can I have more than one unpaid order?**
One unpaid order at a time.

**Where do I manage addresses?**
Profile → My addresses, or add one during checkout.

## 7. Orders, cancellations and tracking

**Where are my orders?**
Profile → My orders. Each order shows its packages and their status.

**Can I cancel an order?**
- Unpaid order: yes, any time.
- Paid package: yes, **until the seller ships it**. You get your money back, including the delivery fee for that package.
- After shipping: you cannot cancel; use the return process if it qualifies (Section 8).

**Can a seller cancel?**
Yes, before shipping. You are refunded.

**How do refunds work?**
Refunds go back through the original payment provider. If the provider has a temporary problem the refund stays pending and is retried automatically. (Online payments are not live yet in the trial, so refunds apply once payments are on.)

## 8. Returns and Buyer Protection

**Can I return an item?**
Yes, within **7 days of delivery**, but only if it arrived **damaged or fake**. Size, fit or change-of-mind returns are **not accepted** because items are pre-owned.

**How do I request a return?**
Open the delivered order and request a return. Choose the reason and **add photos**.

**What happens next?**
1. The seller has **3 days** to accept or reject. If the seller does not respond, the return is accepted automatically.
2. If the seller rejects, you can **escalate to Reloved**; the admin decision is final.
3. If approved, you ship the item back (you have 7 days to do so) and the seller confirms receipt (7 days).
4. You are refunded once the process completes.

**What do I get refunded?**
- The item price.
- Buyer Protection fee, in proportion to the returned items.
- Return shipping, up to ₹99 (₹59 for pickup, ₹0 for meet and collect), charged to the seller.
- The **original delivery fee is not refunded** on returns.
- Coupon use is not given back.

**Can I file a return again after a decision?**
No. A decided return cannot be refiled.

**What is Buyer Protection?**
When you pay through Reloved, a problem such as a damaged or fake item can be raised and decided by Reloved if the seller disagrees. Paying or arranging deals outside the app means you lose this protection.

## 9. Selling on Reloved

**How do I become a seller?**
Tap the **Sell** tab and start the application:
1. **Business details:** business name, type, PAN, address, phone (and GSTIN if you have one).
2. **Documents:** PAN card, address proof (Aadhaar, utility bill, rent agreement and similar) and bank proof. A **GST certificate** is needed only if you enter a GSTIN.
3. **Bank account:** account holder, account number, IFSC.
4. Submit for review.

**How long does approval take?**
Reloved reviews every application. No fixed timeline is promised. The Sell tab shows your status.

**What do the application statuses mean?**
Submitted (under review), changes requested (fix what is asked and resubmit), approved (you can list), rejected, suspended. You can edit details only while the application is a draft or when changes are requested.

**Validation errors on the form**
PAN must look like ABCDE1234F. GSTIN must be 15 characters and its middle must match your PAN. IFSC must look like ABCD0123456. The form highlights the exact field to fix.

**How do I list an item?**
After approval: Sell → My products → New product. Add title, description, category, brand (optional), condition, price, GST rate, options such as sizes, stock, and photos. Photos from a phone are shrunk automatically before upload.

**Do new listings go live immediately?**
New products are reviewed by Reloved before going live. Edits to a live product wait as a "pending change" while the live version keeps selling. Price and stock changes apply instantly.

**What does it cost to sell?**
A commission, **5% of the item price** by default (before any coupon). Delivery fees paid by the buyer go to the seller with no commission on them. Reloved may set different commission rules for certain categories, sellers or products; the rate on your order is fixed when the order is placed.

**When do I get paid?**
Earnings are held for **14 days from the date of payment**, then become available. **Payouts to bank accounts are not switched on yet (trial)**; the Earnings screen shows your balance but transfers to your bank are not available yet.

**What if a buyer returns an item?**
Your earning for that item is reversed. Return shipping (up to ₹99) is charged to you. Reloved absorbs the commission on refunded items; you are not charged extra for it.

**Can I buy my own items?** No.

**Shipping:** the seller packs and ships each order and updates the order status.

**Taxes:** GST is included in item prices. GST on Reloved's commission and fees, GST TCS and income tax TDS are **not yet applied** and are being confirmed with Reloved's accountant. For tax advice, speak to your own accountant.

## 15. Sample questions and short answers

**Q: Is it safe to buy here?** Sellers are verified, and paying through Reloved gives Buyer Protection for damaged or fake items. Keep everything inside the app.

**Q: Can I bargain?** Offers are not built in. You can message the seller to ask.

**Q: Can I return because it doesn't fit?** No, size returns are not accepted; only damaged or fake items within 7 days.

**Q: How long until my package arrives?** Delivery time depends on the seller and the delivery option; no fixed time is promised. Check the order status.

**Q: Why is there a Buyer Protection fee?** It funds the protection and dispute process for your order. It is shown before you pay.

**Q: Can I sell without GST registration?** Yes. A GST certificate is only needed if you enter a GSTIN.

**Q: When will I get my money as a seller?** Earnings are held 14 days after payment. Bank payouts are not live yet.

**Q: Can I delete my account?** Ask human support.

**Q: Is there a delivery partner or courier tracking?** Not integrated yet; the seller updates order status.
