Delete your Reloved account

You can delete your account and personal data yourself, inside the app or by asking us.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Reloved and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile, then Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my account and enter your password to confirm.

By email

If you cannot sign in, email contact@miasolutions.in from the email address on your account with the subject “Delete my Reloved account”. We will confirm and delete it within 7 days.

What happens

  • Removed straight away: your name, email, phone number, saved addresses, favourites, cart, blocks and active sessions. If you sell, your listings are taken down.
  • Kept, without your name or contact details: order, payment, refund and invoice records, and seller tax records, for as long as the law requires.
  • Messages you sent stay visible to the other person in the conversation, shown as from a deleted user.
  • Deleting is permanent. You can register again later with the same email as a new account.

Before you delete

Deletion is refused while you have an order or return in progress. Finish or cancel it first.
